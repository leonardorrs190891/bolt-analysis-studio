# CJA 2022 — Review on anti-loosening methods for threaded fasteners [REFERENCE ONLY]

**Citation:** "Review on anti-loosening methods for threaded fasteners", *Chinese Journal
of Aeronautics* 35(2) (2022) 3–24.
**DOI:** [10.1016/j.cja.2020.12.038](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cja.2020.12.038) (gold OA)
**PDF:** `pdfs_manual_download/cja2022_antiloosening_review.pdf`

## Status: NO curves digitized (review — secondary data)

Comprehensive survey of anti-loosening approaches: locking devices (wedge washers,
double nuts, nylon inserts, adhesives, safety wire), thread-form modifications
(spiralock-type), and testing standards (Junker DIN 65151, DIN 25201-4, NAS 3350).
Compiles Junker-test comparisons across devices from primary sources.

Use as the source-finder for `locking_device_type` modeling and for the
`core/databases/locking_devices.json` entries — trace any needed curve back to its
primary paper before digitizing (review reproductions are low-resolution).
