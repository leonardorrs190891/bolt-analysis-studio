# Terms for the digitised reference data

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` covers the code of this repository. It does
not cover the experimental data behind the reference curves.

The reference curves under Models/CALIBRATION_AND_VALIDATION/curve_library/ and
BAS_V2_papers/*/digitized_csv/ were digitised by the author from figures and
tables of the published studies cited in the accompanying notes. They are
provided solely to make the comparison reproducible; the underlying experimental
data remain the intellectual property of their original authors and publishers
and are not covered by the licence above.

Every curve names the study it was read from: see the apparatus note and
the digitisation manifest of its source under
`Models/CALIBRATION_AND_VALIDATION/curve_library/`. Cite the original
study, not this repository, when you use one of these measurements.
