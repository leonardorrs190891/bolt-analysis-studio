Calibrate parameters (Ctrl+K)

Bolt Analysis Studio 1.0.0 · Narrated user guide

Listen to the narration · voice: en-US-AndrewNeural

Transcript

Analyse, Calibrate model parameters, or control K. First the program asks which curve to fit against: the paper case the model came from, or a CSV of yours. Then the calibration dialogue opens. On the left, the list of parameters, each with a checkbox and two bounds. The rule is simple and it is what makes everything work: what you do not tick stays exactly at the value in the model. That is how you lock what has already been measured. What you tick is searched between the lower and the upper bound. Tick few at a time. On the right, the preview shows the experimental curve, the current model and the candidate fit. Apply writes the result into the model; Discard leaves no trace. The engine that fits is the same one that runs the analysis: what closes here is reproduced in the Run.

Every control on this screen

Parameter / Lo / HiThe box locks or frees; Lo and Hi are the search bounds.
Live PreviewExperimental, current model and fit; MAE and RMSE at the top.
Preview Current / Run Calibration / CancelDraws the current model; starts the fit; interrupts.
Apply to Model / Apply & Re-run / DiscardWrites to the model; writes and runs the analysis; discards.
Common mistake. A fit that closes well against a single curve is not an adopted configuration of the paper: those were fixed per rig and cross-validated. Treat your fit as a hypothesis.
