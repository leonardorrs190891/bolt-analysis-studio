The new-analysis wizard

Bolt Analysis Studio 1.0.0 · Narrated user guide

Listen to the narration · voice: en-US-AndrewNeural

Transcript

The wizard builds a model in five pages. On the first, you name the project and choose the joint topology: single axial bolt, single shear joint, flange, and so on. Each topology already carries the right elements, including the two bearing connections, under the head and under the nut, which are where loosening happens. On the second page, the bolt size and grade, the flange thickness and the preload as a fraction of yield. On the third, the loading: transverse, axial or combined, displacement- or force-controlled, with amplitude, frequency and number of cycles. The fourth is optional: a laboratory CSV to calibrate against later. The fifth summarises everything. Click generate model and it opens in the Model module.

Every control on this screen

ControlWhat it does
Project nameName of the generated model; empty uses the default.
Joint typeList of topologies; the description and the generated elements appear below.
Bolt sizeM6 to M42; diameter and pitch follow automatically.
Material gradeSets yield and ultimate strength, and with them the preload in newtons.
Flange thickness (each)Thickness of each clamped member.
Preload (% yield)Fraction of yield applied at tightening. Seventy percent is the VDI 2230 typical.
Loading typeTransverse Junker, axial pulsating or combined.
Control modeImposed displacement, as on a Junker rig, or imposed force, as on a servo-hydraulic one. The field that is not the driver is greyed out.
Transverse amplitude δPeak displacement, in millimetres. Typical Junker: 0.3 to 1.0.
Axial force amplitudePeak dynamic force, in newtons.
Frequency / CyclesTest frequency and how many cycles to simulate.
Reference CSVOptional: laboratory curve for calibration.
Generate modelMaterialises the chain and opens the Model module.
