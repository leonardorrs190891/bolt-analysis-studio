Glossary

Bolt Analysis Studio 1.0.0 · The technical terms used in this guide, in the program and in the paper, with their Portuguese equivalent. Module names and the Run, Stop and Step buttons stay in English in both languages: they are proper names of the interface, as in Abaqus.

TermIn Portuguese Definition
acceptance criterion (three legs)critério de aceitação (três pernas)MAE, maximum residual and residual spread, each with its own limit.
alignmentalinhamentoDivision of the model by its value at the first measured cycle; the raw MAE is reported beside it.
bearing connection (Bearing Head, Bearing Nut)ligação de apoio (Bearing Head, Bearing Nut)Interface under the head or under the nut; where the face slips, embeds and wears.
bolt sizebitolaNominal metric diameter, such as M8 or M12; defines pitch and areas.
bolted jointjunta aparafusadaThe assembly of bolt, nut and clamped members, with the interfaces between them.
bounds Lo / Hilimites Lo / HiLowest and highest value the calibration may give a parameter.
calibrationcalibraçãoSearch for the ticked parameters, within their bounds, that minimise the error against the reference curve.
censuscensoThe comparable curves that enter the paper's numbers.
clamped membersmembros apertadosThe parts compressed between head and nut: flanges, plates, gasket.
creepfluênciaSlow deformation of the members under constant load, relaxing the preload over time.
cyclecicloOne full oscillation of the excitation; the horizontal axis of the curves.
damping camortecimento cViscous resistance to motion, in newton-seconds per metre; small effect on quasi-static loosening.
decomposition by mechanismdecomposição por mecanismoHow much of the loss came from embedding, creep, wear and nut rotation.
displacement- / force-controlledcontrole por deslocamento / por forçaWhich quantity the machine imposes; the other one follows and is greyed out.
embeddingassentamentoFlattening of the asperities at the interfaces in the first cycles; the dominant mechanism at the start of the curve.
flangeflangeA stiff clamped member; a plate or a pipe flange.
friction coefficient μcoeficiente de atrito μRatio of tangential to normal force at an interface; initial at the thread and under the head.
gasketgaxetaA flexible clamped sealing member; an order of magnitude softer than the flange.
headcabeçaThe end of the bolt that bears on the first member.
intakeintakeThe flow that brings your test in: prompt to an AI, .bascase.json file back, import.
Junker testensaio JunkerThe standard transverse-vibration test for self-loosening.
MAE, mean absolute errorMAE, erro médio absolutoMean of the absolute difference between model and data, as a fraction of F₀.
mass mmassa mMass of the element, in kilograms.
mass-spring-damper chain (MSD)cadeia massa-mola-amortecedor (MSD)Representation of the joint as bodies with mass and stiffness joined by interfaces; the program's model.
material gradeclasse do materialSteel designation, such as 8.8, 10.9 or A193 B7, which fixes Sy and Su.
modulemóduloEach stage of the bar: Model, Contacts, Loads, Analysis, Results, Report.
nutporcaThe threaded element that closes the joint; its rotation is the loosening.
nut rotationrotação da porcaRelative turning between nut and bolt that converts slip into loss of clamp.
pitchpassoDistance between consecutive threads.
preloadpré-cargaTensile force installed in the bolt by tightening; the quantity the program tracks cycle by cycle.
preload lossperda de pré-cargaDrop of the preload along the test, measured as a fraction of F₀.
prompt linelinha de promptThe sentence at the base of the window saying what to do now.
provenanceprocedênciaWhere each adopted constant came from: measurement, literature or fit.
reference curvecurva de referênciaExperimental data, from the paper or from a CSV of yours, against which the model is compared or fitted.
residualresíduoDifference between model and data at one cycle.
self-looseningafrouxamento (autoafrouxamento)Preload loss by nut rotation under cyclic load, with nobody undoing the bolt.
shankhasteThe plain length of the bolt between head and thread; the main spring of the chain.
StepStepAnalysis type: Static-Preload, the static preload, or Coupled-Loosening, the coupled loosening.
stiffness krigidez kForce per displacement of the element, in newtons per metre.
stress area A_sárea resistente A_sEffective thread section used to convert stress into force.
threadroscaThe threaded length of the bolt, engaged in the nut.
thread contactcontato de roscaInterface between the bolt and nut threads.
three-phase friction modelmodelo de atrito em três fasesRun-in, peak and plateau of the coefficient along the cycles; the validated default.
transverse amplitude δamplitude transversal δPeak displacement imposed between the members, in millimetres; the excitation of the Junker test.
ultimate strength Suruptura SuThe highest stress the steel withstands.
V2 enginemotor V2The paper's non-linear solver; the same one in Run and in calibration.
validation casecaso da validaçãoA published curve with the adopted model and the provenance of every constant.
VDI 2230VDI 2230German guideline for bolted-joint design; the origin of seventy percent of yield as the typical preload.
viewportviewportThe central area where the chain is drawn.
washerarruelaRing between the nut or head and the member; changes the bearing friction.
weardesgasteRemoval of material at the slipping faces, proportional to the friction work.
yield strength Syescoamento SyStress at which the steel starts to deform permanently; the basis of the nominal preload.
Young's modulus Emódulo de elasticidade EStiffness of the material; with area and length, gives the element's k.
