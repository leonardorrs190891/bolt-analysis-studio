Results — reading the result

Bolt Analysis Studio 1.0.0 · Narrated user guide

Listen to the narration · voice: en-US-AndrewNeural

Transcript

In Results, the Run tab shows what the analysis produced. The main plot is preload against cycles: the curve the bolt described. Below, the decomposition by mechanism: how much of the loss came from embedding, how much from creep, how much from wear, how much from nut rotation. That decomposition is what tells you whether the joint lost force at the start, from surface embedding, or along the test, from wear. There are also the friction and phase plots. The context bar above the plot switches between plot families without leaving the module.
